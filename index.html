The Platform

One integrated workspace for global seismic monitoring.

Every minute, waveforms from thousands of stations stream into our processing pipeline, where events are detected, located, and reviewed — then published to the same tools our own analysts use. No installs, no license fees: the full platform runs in your browser.

Overview

A single screen that answers "what is happening right now?" — event counts and trends for any period from 24 hours to 10 years, magnitude distribution, the largest events, live global activity, and system health.

Live Map

Global seismicity on an interactive 2D map — or the Earthquake 4D globe in 3D, replaying 126 years of the catalog in space and depth. Pick any date range, filter by magnitude and depth, and open any event for its full solution.

Earthquake Guide

1. Who we are and what this covers

This policy covers the website worldearthquakelabs.com and everything under it, including the 3D globe, the console and the guide pages ("the site"). The site is operated by World Earthquake Labs, reachable at parkman000718@gmail.com. Read it together with our Terms of Service.

2. What we collect

Messages you send us. The site has no registration, login, comment or contact form. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments. We use this information to respond to your request and retain relevant correspondence as needed to handle it. Please do not send information that is unnecessary for your enquiry.

Hosting and request data. Cloudflare delivers the site and may process technical details such as your IP address, the requested page or file, request time, browser and operating system ("user agent"), and referring page. These details support delivery, security and performance. Cloudflare's processing and retention are described in its privacy policy; we do not promise a fixed deletion period for provider-controlled logs.

No analytics. The site does not load Google Analytics or any other audience-measurement or tracking script.

3. Preferences kept in your browser

To remember a few choices between visits, the site stores small settings in your browser's local storage: the display language, the time-zone you picked, dismissed notices, and the state of some controls. These values never leave your device, are not sent to us, and are not used for tracking. You can clear them at any time through your browser's site-data settings; the site works without them. The site itself does not set cookies.

4. Advertising

Advertising is currently disabled: the site does not load Google AdSense or request ads. We may enable Google AdSense in the future. Before doing so, we will put applicable consent controls in place and update this policy. If advertising is enabled, the following disclosures apply:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or to other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google My Ad Center. You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices or, in Europe, at www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Before serving personalised ads in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we will use a Google-certified consent management platform to request consent and provide a way to change or withdraw it. We will obtain consent for cookies or other device storage wherever required. No advertising consent message is currently shown because advertising is disabled.
  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and its advertising technologies at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Ad blockers do not break the site; every feature works with advertising blocked.

6. Links to other sites

The site links to agencies, journals and news publishers. Once you follow a link, that site's privacy policy applies, not this one.

7. Children

The site is a general-audience educational resource and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal information can contact us to request its removal.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority. For information you have sent us or questions about request data, write to us. We will assess the request and may need information to verify that it concerns your own data. For data processed independently by a service provider, consult its policy linked above. If Google advertising is enabled, use the controls in section 4 or Google's My Ad Center.

9. Security and hosting

The site is served over HTTPS and hosted on Cloudflare's network. It has no visitor-account database. Hosting providers process request data, and our email provider stores messages sent to the contact address. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

If the site starts collecting something it does not collect today, this page will say so before it happens, and the date at the top will change.

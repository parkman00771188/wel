1. Who we are and what this covers

This policy covers the website worldearthquakelabs.com and everything under it, including the 3D globe, the console and the guide pages ("the site"). The site is operated by World Earthquake Labs, reachable at parkman000718@gmail.com. Read it together with our Terms of Service.

2. What we collect

Nothing you type. The site has no registration, login, comment, search-log or contact form. If you email us, we keep the correspondence for as long as it takes to deal with it.

Hosting logs. Like every website, the servers that deliver the site record technical details of each request: the IP address it came from, the page or file requested, the date and time, the browser and operating system ("user agent") and the page that linked to it. The site is delivered by Cloudflare, whose network keeps these logs briefly to run the service, block abuse and measure performance. We do not combine them with any other information and we do not use them to identify visitors.

No analytics. The site does not load Google Analytics or any other audience-measurement or tracking script.

3. Preferences kept in your browser

To remember a few choices between visits, the site stores small settings in your browser's local storage: the display language, the time-zone you picked, dismissed notices, and the state of some controls. These values never leave your device, are not sent to us, and are not used for tracking. You can clear them at any time through your browser's site-data settings; the site works without them. The site itself does not set cookies.

Ad blockers do not break the site; every feature works with advertising blocked.

6. Links to other sites

The site links to agencies, journals and news publishers. Once you follow a link, that site's privacy policy applies, not this one.

7. Children

The site is a general-audience educational resource and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; since the site collects no personal information beyond hosting logs, there is nothing to submit.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Because the site holds no personal data beyond short-lived hosting logs that we cannot tie to a person, there is normally nothing for us to retrieve or delete; if you believe otherwise, write to us and we will look into it. For data processed by Google in connection with advertising, use the controls in section 4 or Google's My Ad Center.

9. Security and hosting

The site is served over HTTPS only and hosted on Cloudflare's network. It runs no database of visitors and stores nothing about you on our side, which is the most effective security measure there is.

10. Changes to this policy

If the site starts collecting something it does not collect today, this page will say so before it happens, and the date at the top will change.
